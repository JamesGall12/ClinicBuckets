Questions clinics ask

You work with other clinics. Will our information end up with a competitor?+

No. Everything I see in your clinic stays with your clinic, and that's written into our contract before I start. I never share one clinic's numbers, systems or plans with another. Market research only ever appears anonymised and across the whole market.

Will this replace anyone on my team?+

No. We build systems your team runs. The repetitive chasing gets automated, so your staff spend their time on the patients who need a person. It makes their day easier, not their job smaller.

We already have a marketing agency. Why would we need you?+

Most agencies don't know cannabis. It's one of the most restricted things you can market in the UK: prescription only medicines can't be promoted to the public under MHRA rules, the CQC (or HIW in Wales) regulates how your clinic operates, and Google won't run your ads without LegitScript certification and strict healthcare policy compliance. One wrong word in an ad can get the account suspended. You need someone who runs campaigns at agency level and knows these rules inside out. And where an agency stops at the hose, we also fix the opening and the holes, so every pound goes further.

Is it compliant with UK rules?+

Everything is built for the UK: consent based messaging under GDPR, no product promotion in patient communications, and Google Ads set up for LegitScript certification. Every follow up is logged, so you have a clear record of how patients are looked after.

Your results are from Australia. Will it work in the UK?+

The patient journey is the same: enquire, book, pay, attend, come back for the next script. Our study of 24 UK clinics found the same leaks here, often worse.

Do you need access to patient data?+

We work inside your existing systems alongside your team, so patient data stays where it is. An NDA is signed before we start.

Which CRM do you work with?+

The one you already have. We build around your setup rather than asking you to switch.

Can we just do Google Ads?+

Yes. Google Ads is available on its own, including getting through LegitScript certification.

How long before we see a difference?+

Setup takes four weeks. Tracking goes in first, so you see the before and after in your own numbers.

What does it cost?+

It depends on your clinic's size and what needs fixing. You'll get a fixed price after the first call, before anything starts. The audit and fix plan can be bought on its own.
